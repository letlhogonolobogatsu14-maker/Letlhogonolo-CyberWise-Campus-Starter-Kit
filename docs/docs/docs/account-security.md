Introduction:Keeping your account secure is very important as it keeps your personal details, academic details and online accounts safe from hackers.
Password Security:Use a strong, long and unique password for each account. Use a password manager.
Multi-Factor Authentication:This adds another layer of security to your account. You will be asked for another form of identification (code/fingerprint).
Updating Software:This helps to fix any security issues and keeps your device safe from viruses, malware and cyber attacks.
Keeping Devices Safe:Use a screen lock, encryption and only download from trusted sources.
Conclusion:Students should use good cyberscurity practices. Use strong passwords, turn MFA on, keep your devices updated and avoid any suspicious links.
